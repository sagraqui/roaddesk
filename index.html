Why crews use it

The production office, in your pocket.

On a tour, the same forty questions get asked a hundred times a day. RoadDesk takes them off the tour manager's phone.

📖

Grounded in the tour book

Answers come from the official tour book and crew policies, not from guesses. If it is not in the book, RoadDesk says so.

⏱

Instant schedule answers

Load-in, sound check, doors, curfew and bus call for any date on the leg, without scrolling a 60-page PDF.

📞

Key contacts on tap

Who to call for travel, payroll, safety or a missing laminate. One tap from the chat's Key contacts button.

📋

Policy without the lecture

Per diem, guest list, overtime and safety rules explained in plain language, with the policy behind each answer.

🌙

Built for 3 a.m.

Dark by default, thumb friendly, and awake when the production office is finally asleep on the bus.

🚀

Live in a day

Upload the tour book, review the answers, share one link. No app install and no crew training required.
